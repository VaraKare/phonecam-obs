PhoneCam is installed in OBS.

PhoneCam also needs Android platform-tools (adb), a free tool from Google:

  • with Homebrew: brew install android-platform-tools, or
  • download “SDK Platform-Tools for Mac” from developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools and unzip it into ~/Library/Android/sdk/platform-tools.

Then open OBS and choose Sources › + › PhoneCam (phone camera).
