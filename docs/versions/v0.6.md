# PhoneCam v0.6: control from OBS, microphone, screen sharing

Phone app and OBS plugin 0.6.0-alpha.1 (app versionCode 14). The phone app also works with older plugins (video only). On Windows, keep the v0.4.0 plugin until the 0.6 plugin has been tested on a Windows PC.

## What's new

### Control the phone from OBS

- While live, the PhoneCam source's **Properties** show a **Phone** section: a line like "Rear camera · USB · battery 80 %", then **Camera** and **Quality** lists, **Torch** and **Mirror** tick boxes, and **Stop live on the phone**. A heat or battery warning shows there too.
- OBS → Settings → **Hotkeys** has three PhoneCam keys: switch phone camera, torch on/off, stop live on the phone.
- OBS can't *start* live: only the phone's record button does (owner's rule, for privacy).
- OBS lists Properties one per row, so the section is kept short: two lists, two tick boxes and a button.

### Phone microphone (optional)

- The **microphone icon** on the main screen (blue when on, crossed out when off). Android asks for permission only when you turn it on. It can be turned on and off while live.
- The sound arrives in OBS on the PhoneCam source (Audio Mixer), with the picture and sound timed by the phone's own clock so they stay together. OBS's **Sync Offset** (Advanced Audio Properties) can fine-tune it.
- With an older OBS plugin the phone stops with a clear message rather than sending video without the sound you asked for.
- A PhoneCam source created before 0.6 had no audio tracks. The plugin turns tracks 1–6 on the first time the microphone sends sound (once; your own later choice is kept).

### Screen sharing

- The **screen icon** next to the camera icon. Tap the record button, then allow Android's screen-sharing prompt (it asks every time). On Android 14 and later the prompt offers "A single app" or "Entire screen"; some phones hide the choice.
- The screen goes to OBS up to 1080p through the same connection. Turning the phone resizes it without leaving OBS. The microphone works with it; the phone's own sound is for a later version.
- It keeps going when you leave PhoneCam (that's the point), with the notification and Stop.

### Looks after the phone

- **Heat:** a notification when the phone gets warm and again when hot, with **Lower quality** (Best → High → Balanced).
- **Battery:** a notification at 20, 15, 10 and 5 % while live and not charging.
- **Live ended while away:** if live stops while PhoneCam isn't on screen (OBS closed, cable out), a notification with the reason, with sound and vibration.
- **Dim screen:** after 30 seconds of live without a touch, the phone screen dims; a touch brightens it (the first touch only wakes it).
- Each one can be turned off in **Settings → While live**.

### Smoother

- **USB preferred:** with a cable to a computer plugged in, USB gets 2 seconds to connect before Wi-Fi is used.
- **Faster start:** the camera opens while the encoder is being set up. The phone writes its start timings into the OBS log ("Phone start timings: …").

### Main screen

- One row of icons: camera, screen | quality, mirror, microphone. **Press and hold any icon to see its name.** Settings → **What the buttons do** lists them all.
- The line under the icons shows the quality, camera and settings, for example "High · Rear camera · H.265 · 20 Mb/s".
- The top status: a grey dot, a cable icon with "USB" when plugged into a computer, and a Wi-Fi icon with the paired computer's name when on Wi-Fi. Amber while waiting for OBS, red while live.
- The record button is round with no text (v0.5); the phone's messages say "record button".

## Rules this version adds

From the owner, 21 November 2026, recorded in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and [PLAN.md](../PLAN.md): OBS may control a live session but never start one; the microphone stays off unless the user turns it on and is refused rather than silently dropped; screen sharing only after Android's consent each time, always with the notification.

## How it works

- **Two-way link:** a 0.6 plugin sends a short "hello" after connecting; a 0.6 phone answers. Then the phone sends status and audio, and OBS sends commands. Older plugins and phones skip this and get video only. Over Wi-Fi, OBS's messages use their own encryption keys. Details: [USB live](../USB_LIVE.md#two-way-link-06) and [Wi-Fi](../WIFI.md).
- **Audio:** 48 kHz, 16-bit, mono, in 10 ms chunks, stamped on the same clock as the camera frames. OBS maps the phone's clock to its own with one offset (the least delayed message, renewed every 30 s).
- **Screen:** Android's MediaProjection draws the screen into the same hardware encoder the camera uses; a still screen repeats its last picture so OBS stays fed.

## Tested on hardware

vivo V2437 (Android 16) with OBS 33.0.1 on an Apple M1 Mac, 21–22 November 2026. Details: [research note](../research/VIVO_V2437_V06_TESTS.md).

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| New app with the older 0.4.1 plugin | First build: kept disconnecting (it sent a new message type to the old plugin); fixed, then video worked and the microphone was refused with the message |
| OBS controls (camera, quality, torch, mirror, stop) | Worked; then made shorter (lists and tick boxes) and checked again |
| Stop from OBS | First: OBS said "video stalled"; fixed, then "the phone stopped sending" |
| Start timings (USB) | OBS connected 0.66 s, camera open 1.22 s, first frame 1.70 s after the tap (before the faster start) |
| Microphone | See the research note |
| Screen sharing | Worked (the phone's prompt offered one app) |
| Warnings, alert when away, USB preferred, dimming, icons, top status | Owner: all worked |

## Not verified yet

- The 0.6 plugin on a Windows PC.
- Screen sharing of the entire screen, and on other phones.
- Long sessions with the microphone (clock drift over an hour).
- Phones other than the vivo V2437.
