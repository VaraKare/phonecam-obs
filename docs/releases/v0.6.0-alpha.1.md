Control the phone from OBS, use the phone's microphone, share the phone's screen, and get warnings before the phone overheats or runs flat.

**Preview release.** Tested on one phone (vivo V2437, Android 16) and one Mac (Apple M1, OBS 33.0.1). Other Android 10+ phones are expected to work but aren't verified yet. Please send a Device report (Issues → New issue) either way.

**Windows:** keep the [v0.4.0-alpha.1](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/releases/tag/v0.4.0-alpha.1) plugin. The 0.6 Windows plugin passes the automatic checks but hasn't run on a Windows PC yet. The new phone app works with it (video only, as before).

## What's new

- **Control from OBS.** While live, the PhoneCam source's Properties show a **Phone** section: camera, quality, torch, mirror and Stop, plus the phone's battery. OBS hotkeys can switch camera, toggle the torch and stop. Starting live stays on the phone.
- **Phone microphone (optional).** Tap the microphone icon (blue = on). Android asks permission only then. The sound arrives on the PhoneCam source in OBS, in time with the picture.
- **Share the phone screen** instead of the camera: tap the screen icon, then the record button, and allow Android's prompt (it asks every time). Up to 1080p, through the same cable or Wi-Fi.
- **Heat and battery warnings:** a notification when the phone gets hot, with one-tap **Lower quality**, and at 20, 15, 10 and 5 % battery.
- **Never ends silently:** if live stops while you're in another app, a notification says why.
- **Smoother:** USB is preferred when the cable is in, the camera opens faster, and the screen dims after 30 s without a touch while live.
- **New look:** one row of icons (camera, screen, quality, mirror, microphone). Press and hold any icon to see its name; Settings → What the buttons do lists them all. The top status shows a cable icon for USB and a Wi-Fi icon with your computer's name.

Every new option can be turned off in Settings → While live. Full guide: [docs/versions/v0.6.md](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/blob/main/docs/versions/v0.6.md).

## Upgrading: one step for the microphone

A PhoneCam source you added before 0.6 had no sound, so OBS gave it no audio tracks and warns it "is not assigned to any audio tracks". Open **Edit → Advanced Audio Properties**, tick **Tracks 1–6** for the PhoneCam source, and unmute it in the Audio Mixer. Once is enough; the next update does it by itself.

## Install

- **Phone:** `PhoneCam-0.6.0-alpha.1.apk`. It updates earlier versions in place and keeps your pairings.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, OBS 33):** `PhoneCam-OBS-plugin-macos-arm64-0.6.0-alpha.1.zip`. Quit OBS first, then follow its INSTALL.txt or the [README](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs#install-one-time).

Check your downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
