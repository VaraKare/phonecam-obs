Windows is back, one-click installers for Mac and Windows, the phone's own sound in screen sharing, and OBS recording that starts and stops with the phone.

**Preview release.** Tested on one phone (vivo V2437, Android 16), one Mac (Apple M1, OBS 33.0.2) and one Windows laptop. Other Android 10+ phones are expected to work but aren't verified yet. Please send a Device report (Issues → New issue) either way.

## What's new

- **Windows plugin with an installer.** The same features as on the Mac, tested on a Windows laptop over USB and Wi-Fi. Run `…-setup.exe` with OBS closed (**More info → Run anyway**: the preview isn't signed). Uninstall from Windows **Settings → Apps**.
- **One-click Mac installer (.pkg).** No Terminal step. It replaces an older PhoneCam plugin by itself. It's unsigned: right-click → **Open**.
- **Recording follows the phone.** In the source's Properties → **Recording**:
  - **Stop OBS recording when the phone stops live** (on). Only a deliberate Stop counts: a pulled cable never stops your recording.
  - **Start OBS recording when the phone goes live** (off).
- **Better screen sharing:**
  - choose **Entire screen** or **one app** (Android 14+);
  - send the **phone's own sound**: the speaker icon, off until you turn it on;
  - steady 30 fps.
- **Ready for OBS updates:** works with OBS 33.0 and every newer version. An automatic weekly check tries PhoneCam in the newest OBS.
- **Smoother:**
  - the first picture in under 1.5 s over USB;
  - clean pop-ups;
  - a once-a-minute health line in the OBS log;
  - Wi-Fi and Wireless-debugging fixes.

Full guide: [docs/versions/v0.7.md](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/blob/main/docs/versions/v0.7.md).

## Good to know

- **No sound from the phone in OBS?** Open **Edit → Advanced Audio Properties**, tick **Tracks 1–6** for the PhoneCam source, and unmute it. Once is enough.
- **Windows: put adb at `C:\platform-tools`.** Unzip Google's SDK Platform-Tools and move the `platform-tools` folder to `C:\`.
- **All those "Allow" prompts** (Windows SmartScreen, the admin prompt, "Allow USB debugging" on the phone, the firewall for Wi-Fi) are Windows and Android checking with you. PhoneCam has no account and no analytics, and sends nothing to the internet. Wi-Fi goes only to the computer you paired, on your own network.
- **4K on a laptop** can drop frames. 1080p (High) is the safe choice.

## Tested on real hardware

The phone with the Mac and with a Windows laptop. Passed on both:
- installers;
- USB;
- recording options;
- screen sharing with phone sound;
- the microphone.

Also passed on Windows: Wi-Fi pairing and live, cable pull while recording, replug, hide/show and quitting OBS.

Not yet verified: tablets, other phones, Intel Macs, long runs. See the [Mac](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/blob/main/docs/research/V07_MAC_TESTS.md) and [Windows](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/blob/main/docs/research/WINDOWS_V07_TEST.md) test records.

## Install

- **Phone:** `PhoneCam-0.7.0-alpha.1.apk`. It updates earlier versions in place and keeps your pairings.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, OBS 33 or newer):** `PhoneCam-OBS-plugin-macos-arm64-0.7.0-alpha.1.pkg`, or the `.zip`.
- **Windows (64-bit, OBS 33 or newer):** `PhoneCam-OBS-plugin-windows-x64-0.7.0-alpha.1-setup.exe`.

Quit OBS before installing a plugin. Step by step: [README](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs#install-one-time). Check your downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
