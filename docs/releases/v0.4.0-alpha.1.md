Second public preview of PhoneCam OBS: your Android phone's camera as a video source in OBS Studio, now also on Windows (in testing), with a redesigned phone screen.

**Preview release.** Tested on one phone (vivo V2437, Android 16), one Mac (Apple M1, OBS 33.0.1) and one Windows laptop (Windows 11, OBS 33). Please send a Device report (Issues → New issue) either way.

## What's new

- **Windows (preview):** OBS plugin with hardware decoding (Direct3D 11), USB and direct Wi-Fi with QR pairing. *Owner to decide how it ships; see "Before publishing".*
- **Redesigned phone screen:**
  - quality and mirror chips;
  - a switch-camera button beside a round Start/Stop;
  - switch camera or quality while live, with no need to stop first;
  - a live timer and route on the badge ("● Live 05:23 · USB").
- **Paired computers** in Settings, with a count, and Forget for each computer.
- **Plain-language errors** when pairing or connecting fails.
- **Keep the phone live while the source is hidden** (an OBS option), for quick scene switching.
- **OBS Properties in sections:** a status box (yellow when something needs you), Connection, Picture, Advanced, with short help texts.
- **Fixes:**
  - the first Start after opening OBS no longer drops;
  - hiding a source waits at most 0.5 s;
  - the heat chip follows the phone after Stop;
  - the expired QR card hides itself;
  - Wi-Fi pairing works twice in a row;
  - OBS closes in seconds.
