Second public preview of PhoneCam OBS: your Android phone's camera as a video source in OBS Studio. Now with a **Windows preview** and a redesigned phone screen.

**Preview release.** Tested on one phone (vivo V2437, Android 16), one Mac (Apple M1, OBS 33.0.1) and one Windows 11 laptop (OBS 33). Other Android 10+ phones are expected to work but aren't verified yet. Please send a Device report (Issues → New issue) either way.

## What's new

- **Windows preview:** OBS plugin with hardware decoding (Direct3D 11), over USB and direct Wi-Fi with QR pairing. This is the exact build tested on a Windows laptop. It still has the older OBS Properties layout.
- **Redesigned phone screen:**
  - quality and mirror chips;
  - a switch-camera button beside a round Start/Stop;
  - a live timer and route on the badge ("● Live 05:23 · USB").
- **Switch camera or quality while live.** The picture pauses for about 3 s and continues; OBS keeps recording.
- **Paired computers** in the phone's Settings, with Forget for each one.
- **Plain-language errors.** For example, on college or office Wi-Fi that keeps devices apart, the phone suggests its hotspot.
- **Keep the phone live while the OBS source is hidden** (an option), for quick scene switching.
- **Tidier OBS Properties on the Mac:** a status line that turns yellow when something needs you, then Connection, Picture and Advanced sections with short help.
- **Fixes:**
  - the first Start after opening OBS no longer drops;
  - hiding a source is quicker;
  - the heat chip clears after Stop;
  - an expired QR card hides itself;
  - Wi-Fi pairing works repeatedly;
  - OBS closes in seconds.

## Tested on real hardware

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Mac: recording in OBS while switching camera | One 3.3 s pause, no black frames, recording continued |
| Mac: 1080p → 4K → 1080p while recording | 4K (3840×2160) with hardware decoding; same fit after each change |
| Mac: direct Wi-Fi over the phone's hotspot | QR pairing and live 1080p, including camera and quality changes |
| Mac: Paired computers, errors, OBS status and help | Working |
| Windows laptop: USB, replug, hide/show, Wi-Fi pairing twice, Wi-Fi live | Working; 13-minute 1080p run with no late frames; OBS closes in 3 s |

Not yet verified: 4K on Windows (about 24 fps on the test laptop; 1080p is smooth), Smooth 60 fps, sessions over 13 minutes, measured latency, phones other than the vivo V2437.

## Install

- **Phone:** `PhoneCam-0.4.0-alpha.1.apk`. It updates v0.3 in place and keeps your pairings.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, OBS 33):** `PhoneCam-OBS-plugin-macos-arm64-0.4.0-alpha.1.zip`. Follow its INSTALL.txt or the [README](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs#install-one-time).
- **Windows (64-bit, OBS 33):** `PhoneCam-OBS-plugin-windows-x64-0.4.0-alpha.1-preview.zip`. Follow its INSTALL.txt.

Check your downloads against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
