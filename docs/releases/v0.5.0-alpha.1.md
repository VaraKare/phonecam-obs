Live keeps going when you leave PhoneCam, with a notification and picture-in-picture. Also a new record button and Settings during live.

**Preview release.** Tested on one phone (vivo V2437, Android 16) and one Mac (Apple M1, OBS 33.0.1). Other Android 10+ phones are expected to work but aren't verified yet. Please send a Device report (Issues → New issue) either way.

**Phone app only.** The OBS plugins don't change: use the [v0.4.1-alpha.1](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/releases/tag/v0.4.1-alpha.1) Mac plugin and the [v0.4.0-alpha.1](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/releases/tag/v0.4.0-alpha.1) Windows plugin.

## What's new

- **Live when you leave the app (on by default).** Switch apps, go Home or lock the phone, and OBS keeps the picture. A notification shows "● PhoneCam is live to OBS · USB" with a timer and **Stop**, and Android shows its camera dot. Swiping PhoneCam away from recent apps stops live.
- **Picture-in-picture (on by default).** Going Home during live shows the phone preview in a small floating window with **Stop**. Closing the window keeps live running in the background.
- **Record button.** Start and Stop is a round record button like a phone camera: a red circle to go live, a red square to stop.
- **Settings during live.** Most settings can be changed while live; ones that use the camera wait until you stop.
- **Phones that close background apps** (vivo, Xiaomi, OPPO and others): Settings → While live → Let PhoneCam run in the background opens the right Android page, and PhoneCam explains if the phone closed it.

Both new options are in **Settings → While live**. Turning background live off brings back the v0.4 behaviour: live stops when PhoneCam leaves the screen.

The full guide for this version is in [docs/versions/v0.5.md](https://github.com/VaraKare/phonecam-obs/blob/main/docs/versions/v0.5.md).

## Tested on real hardware

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Live, then Home, over Wi-Fi and over USB | Kept going; notification with the route and Stop |
| Stop in the notification | Ended live and cleared the notification |
| Screen locked 30 s | Kept going; preview and timer came back on unlock |
| Swiped away from recent apps | Live stopped |
| Background live turned off | Live stopped on leaving, with a message on how to turn it on |
| Picture-in-picture: window, Stop, closing the window | Window showed the preview; Stop ended live; closing it kept live in the background |
| Record button and Settings during live | Worked |

Not yet verified: long background sessions (battery and heat), the message shown when a phone closes PhoneCam in the background, Android 10 and 11, phones other than the vivo V2437.

## Install

- **Phone:** `PhoneCam-0.5.0-alpha.1.apk`. It updates v0.3 or v0.4 in place and keeps your pairings. The first time you go live, allow notifications so Stop shows in the status bar.

Check your download against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
